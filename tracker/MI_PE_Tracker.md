## 5. PORTFOLIO IMPLICATIONS (10–20 YEAR)

**Durable structural shifts (signal, not noise):**
- **The cheap-leverage era is over — confirmed harder this cycle.** A Warsh Fed openly weighing *hikes* makes the next decade about operational value creation and recurring cash flow, not debt-fueled multiple expansion. Over-weight low-leverage, pricing-power, mandated/recurring-revenue businesses.
- **AI is a two-sided force, and the seam is now visible.** Jun 5 (Broadcom → SOX −10.3%) was the first real air-pocket; the $36B Anthropic private-credit deal shows the boom is increasingly *debt-financed*. Long the **physical enabling layer** (power/grid/thermal — AI tailwind without chip-equity beta or PC-credit risk); fade crowded chip equities and avoid index-level private credit now fattened with AI exposure.
- **Geopolitical fragmentation is structural.** The 2026 Iran War + repeated Hormuz open/close cycles make **energy security, defense industrial base, supply-chain resilience, critical minerals** durable themes — independent of weekly spot oil.

**Positioning:**
- **Core long-term:** energy/grid/electrification + firm power; defense sub-tier; AI picks-and-shovels (HV electrical services, data-center thermal services, nuclear qualified supply chain); mandated-recurring services (PFAS O&M, fire/life-safety inspection); reshoring/industrial niches.
- **Opportunistic (opening now):** distressed/rescue capital into fallen-angel software LBOs as PC stress builds; mid-market carve-outs from corporates refocusing on AI/core; North Asia succession (FX window); selective biotech as the IPO window reopens.
- **Avoid / ignore:** late-entry index-level private credit (now AI-concentrated); highly-levered mega-buyouts priced on rate cuts that aren't coming; top-of-stack/chip AI equity at peak multiples; crowded roll-ups (HVAC, accounting, physician practices); pure-play SMR developers; directional spot-oil bets.
